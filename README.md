Sunny Math Racer v2.0 🏎️➕

A Pixar-inspired educational 3-lane math racing Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for kids ages 6–10. Kids solve addition problems by steering their race car into the correct answer lane to accelerate from a parked start up to 100+ km/hr or mph!

What's New in v2.0

3 Distinct Vehicle Styles: Choose between a sculpted Race Car (#95), a rugged Big Truck (#88), and a sporty Cool SUV (#77), complete with custom two-tone split windshields, rear spoilers, and cargo beds.

Glossy Candy Paint Shop: Customize your ride with Lightning Red, Turbo Blue, or Stealth Black.

Age-Adaptive Difficulty:

Under 6 Yrs: Sums strictly below 10 paired with color-coded visual counting dots right under the math problem.

Ages 6–10: Progressive arithmetic challenge scaling with your speed.

Zero-Speed Start & Speed Science: Start parked at 0 km/hr or 0 mph on the checkered starting line. Accelerate +10 per correct answer and learn everyday speed comparisons (Bicycles, School Buses, Express Trains).

Turbo Boost & Stun Mechanics: Correct answers trigger a 5-second turbo boost; incorrect answers trigger a 3-second slowdown with an instant educational correction (e.g., Oops! 3 + 2 = 5).

PWA Installable: Fully installable on iOS, Android, and desktop devices as a native app with a custom racing car logo.
